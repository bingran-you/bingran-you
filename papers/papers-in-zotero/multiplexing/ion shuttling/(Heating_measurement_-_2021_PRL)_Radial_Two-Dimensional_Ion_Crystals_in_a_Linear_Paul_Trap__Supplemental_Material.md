TRAP GEOMETRY
Experiments are performed with 171Yb+ ions conﬁned in a four-rod linear Paul trap (Fig. 1) with two “needle” endcaps along the zˆ (axial) direction. An oscillating rf voltage V0 is applied to two opposing rods (the other two are grounded), creating a quadrapole potential in the xy (radial) plane, while a static dc voltage U0 is applied to the two endcaps. Radial and axial trap dimensions are given by r0 = 460 µm and z0 = 335 µm, respectively, and the trap drive frequency is Ωt = 2π × 21 MHz. The r-
f voltage V0 is held constant at 340 V throughout experiments, yielding a Mathieu parameter of q = .10, while the dc voltage U0 is varied between .012 − 30 V. The trap geometric factor is determined through frequency measurements to be κ 0.12. As the dc voltage is raised, the aspect ratio α = ωz/ωr increases. At large values of α, ions are squeezed into the xy plane and self-assemble into a triangular lattice.
