Building the first 3D printed trap
• Trap fabrication
– Technology: two-photon polymerization – Metal coating to create isolated electrodes

3D printing: Two-Photon Polymerization
• Photoresist transits to polymer after absorbing two photons from tightly focused pulsed laser.
• Can achieve sub-diffraction resolution of 200nm-1µm, due to high order nonlinear optical process.

Zhou et al., AIP Adv. (2015)

• Easy to print complicated structures • Short turnaround time • Scalable: able to print a large trap network
with multiple traps on one chip

Challenge: electrical isolation

• Single overhang structure had bad isolation yield of nearly 0 • We adopted the double overhang structure for a yield of 90%.
