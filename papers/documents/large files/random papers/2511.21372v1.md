QUALITATIVE PROPERTIES OF SINGLE BLOW-UP SOLUTIONS FOR NONLINEAR HARTREE EQUATION WITH SLIGHTLY SUBCRITICAL EXPONENT
ALESSANDRO CANNONE, SILVIA CINGOLANI†, MINBO YANG‡, AND SHUNNENG ZHAO§
Abstract. In this paper, we study the qualitative properties of single blow-up solutions to the nonlocal equations with slightly subcritical exponents
−∆u = (|x|−(n−2) ∗ up−ε)up−1−ε in Ω, u = 0 on ∂Ω,
where Ω is a smooth bounded domain in Rn for n = 3, 4, 5, ∗ denotes the standard convolution, ε > 0 is a small parameter and p = n+2
n−2 is D1,2 energy-critical exponent. By exploiting various local Pohozaev identities and blow-up analysis, we provide a number of estimates on the first (n + 2)-eigenvalues and their corresponding eigenfunctions, and examine the qualitative behavior of the eigenpairs (λi,ε, vi,ε) to the linearied problem of the above nonlocal equations for i = 1, · · · , n + 2. As a corollary, we derive the Morse index of a single-bubble solution in a nondegenerate setting.

Finally, we would like to mention some related results to our problem. The problem (1.1) can be understood as the nonlocal version of the following problem with nonlinearities of slightly subcritical growth
−∆u = u n+2
n−2 −ε, u > 0, in Ω, u = 0, on ∂Ω, (1.23)
where Ω is a bounded domain in Rn for n ≥ 3 and ε is a small parameter. The studies on the asymptotic behavior of radial solutions of (1.23) were initiated by Atkinson and Peletier in [2] by using ODE technique in the unit ball of R3. Later, Brezis and Peletier [3] used the method of PDE to obtain the same results as that in [2] for the spherical domains. Finally, the same kind of results hold for nonspherical domain, which was settled by Han in [25] (independently by Rey in [36]). Moreover, this -
result was extended in [34], where Musso and Pistoia obtained the existence of multi-peak solutions for certain domains, the elliptic systems in [6, 23] and the fractional cases [7]. (See also [27, 33, 37, 42, 44]). Later, Grossi and Pacella in [22] obtained the asymptotic behavior of the eigenvalues λ for the problem
−∆v = n(n − 2)λup−ε
ε v, v > 0, in Ω, v = 0, on ∂Ω, (1.24)
and the Morse index of uε, as well as their result was extended later to the multi-bubble solutions case in [8]. For one-bubble solutions case, Takahashi in [41] analyzed the linearized Brezis-Nirenberg problem. In [19], Gladiali and Grossi studied the asymptotic behavior of the eigenvalues and Morse index of one-bubble solution for the linearized Gelfand problem. Later that, the corresponding problem of multi-bubble solutions was proved in [21], and further the qualitative properties of the first-

Our results are motivated by the work of Gross and Pacella [22] on the classical local problem (1.24) and it can be viewed as non-local counterpart of the qualitative properties results in [22].

Proof of Corollary 1.6. By virtue of Theorem 1.4 we have that any νi−1 < 0 for i = 2, · · · , n + 1 so that λ1,ε < 1, and λn+2,ε > 1 in (1.21) for ε sufficiently small, we obtain
1 ≤ 1 + ind(−D2φ(x0)) ≤ ind(uε) ≤ n + 1,
and the conclusion follows. □
