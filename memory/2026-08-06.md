### Gotchas hit during the copy (worth remembering)

- The codex worktrees tree (5.9 GB, 58k files) contains repo-tracked
  symlinks (`CLAUDE.md`, `.agents/.claude` skills mirrors). Plain
  robocopy **follows** symlinks and chokes on broken ones; robocopy
  `/SL` (copy links as links) fails with ERROR 1314 when not elevated —
  Developer Mode does not help robocopy. Net effect: symlinks got
  materialized or skipped. That's fine here — they're repo-derivable,
  zero unique data — so L: ended up a superset of H:'s real files
  (58,164 vs 56,911 non-reparse files).
- Bingran exited Codex desktop mid-migration so the sessions/worktrees
  junctions could flip with no open handles. Post-flip delta copies
  were all zero — nothing was written to H: after the switch.
