## History rewrite

Bingran: "rewrite git history to purge the two personal documents". Two non-paper documents had been mirrored into this public repository under the paper mirror. They were deleted from the tree on 2026-10-10 (#638) but stayed in history.

What was done:

- A fresh mirror clone was filtered with `git filter-repo`: both documents removed from every commit (they had lived under three directory names), and the entries naming them removed from the index files of the mirror.
- Checked before pushing: a scan of every object reachable in the rewritten history finds neither the names nor the content; the final tree is identical; across all 691 commits only the two documents and the two index files differ; authors, dates and subjects are unchanged.
- The first 16 commits kept their hashes (the rewrite was limited to what came after them). The other 675 have new hashes and no signature, so they no longer show as verified.
- `main` and the tags `v0.0.0` and `v0.1.0` were force-pushed. The "Main" ruleset forbids that; the admin bypass allowed it and the ruleset was not changed.
- The main checkout was reset to the new `main` (it was clean and its files were identical), so the daily update task works on the new history.

Still open after the push:

- GitHub keeps the old commits reachable through closed pull requests. Only GitHub Support can purge them; that request is Bingran's to send.
- A local branch of the daily update task and any clone elsewhere still hold the old history. They must not be pushed or merged.

## Papers as blocks

Bingran, about the paged layout shipped the day before: "the layout should not have too much blank space for each page, and the paper - paper should be clear instead of mixed. also the reference with list of paper should be at the very last page".

The cause: all abstracts ran on as one two-column flow while figures floated to page tops, so a page showed one paper's figure over the next paper's text and the reference list sat before the last figures.

What changed (`lib/paginate.ts` was rewritten; the procedure is in `personal-site/README.md`):

- A paper is one block: a rule and its title across the page, the citation line, the abstract in two balanced columns, its figure and caption, its table. Nothing of another paper stands between.
- Page breaks are chosen for the whole article at once, then each page's leftover height is shared among the gaps between its blocks (more above a paper than inside one), so pages end at the foot.
- The reference list is the last block. The last sheet ends under it.
- `/about`, `/projects` and `/papers` end under their content instead of being a full page half blank.
- The one figure narrower than a column stands beside its abstract and caption.
- The last paper has its figure before its abstract; the breaks leave no emptier page that way.

What it costs, and what is left:

- Eight sheets instead of seven. The same content holds about a fifth of white, a little more than before, but spread between blocks instead of in holes.
- The first three papers each begin at the foot of a page and continue on the next.
- Beside the narrow figure the left column has about 150 pt of white between abstract and caption.
- Two gaps above a title are wide (about 92 and 95 pt).

## Things worth knowing next time

- `git filter-repo` rewrites every commit from the root because signatures cannot be kept. Limit it with `--refs main <tags> ^<last untouched commit>` to keep the early commits as they are.
- A mirror clone keeps `remote.origin.mirror`, which refuses refspecs. Push to the URL.
- A long background run stalls when the machine idles to sleep. Keep it awake for the run.
