# Welcome

This is a small place on the internet for two tracks of work I care about: building **reliable AI systems**, and running **trapped-ion quantum experiments**. They look different from the outside but rhyme more than you'd think — both are about making complex, noisy systems behave.

## What lives here

- **Projects** — the open-source work and a few applied AI products.
- **Papers** — selected publications.
- **Blog** — short notes on things I learn, screw up, or want to remember.

## Stack

This site is a small Next.js + MDX app, deployed on Vercel. Source on [GitHub](https://github.com/bingran-you/personal-site). It's written so I can drop a new MDX file into `content/posts/` and ship a post.

If you want to reach out, the footer has the usual suspects.
