# What I See Working — and Not — in Zero-Employee Operations

Most posts on this topic are either breathless hype or skeptical takedowns. The honest answer in mid-2026 is &quot;yes, but in a narrower band than the hype suggests, and with specific failure modes you can name.&quot; This is my read of where the [zero-human company](/zero-human-company) and [one-person company](/one-person-company) ideas are actually shipping, where they are partially demonstrated, and where they remain narrative.

I am anchoring this in the public reference points — Paperclip, Andon Market, Project Vend, ClawBank, Coinbase x402, AWS Bedrock AgentCore Payments, Medvi, Coinbase&apos;s one-person teams memo — rather than personal claims. Where I have direct opinion, I will mark it.

## What is genuinely shipping

**Single-product SaaS at the AI-native one-person company mode.** A founder running a small product with agentic content, SEO and GEO, first-line support, and most engineering. The Coinbase one-person teams memo (May 2026) is essentially the same idea inside a public company: combine engineer, designer, and PM into a single AI-native role. This is the most repeatable, well-understood mode in 2026.

**Newsletter, course, info-product, programmatic-SEO, and niche-directory businesses.** Agents handle drafting, ingestion, refresh, and operations; founder handles selection, voice, taste, and approvals. The cost structure does not look like a traditional business of comparable revenue.

**Live experiments where an LLM manages a real business with real money.** Anthropic&apos;s Project Vend put Claude in charge of a small office shop in 2025. Andon Labs&apos; Andon Market signed a three-year retail lease in San Francisco&apos;s Cow Hollow in 2026 and put an AI agent named Luna in charge of product, pricing, hours, and even hiring decisions. Humans handle physical execution; the management layer is software.

**Agent-organisation control planes as a product category.** Paperclip&apos;s open-source positioning — agent org chart, budgets, heartbeats, governance, audit — has crystallised what the operating system for a zero-human company actually looks like. This was muddled twelve months ago; it is much clearer now.

**Agentic payment rails.** Coinbase&apos;s x402 and AWS Bedrock AgentCore Payments give agents a way to discover services, pay micropayments, and continue execution under enterprise-grade governance, compliance, budget controls, and audit. This was the missing layer for any agent doing real commerce.

## What is partially demonstrated

**Agents self-incorporating and holding financial accounts.** ClawBank&apos;s Manfred has demonstrated registering a US LLC, obtaining an EIN, and operating against an FDIC-insured bank account and a crypto wallet through an API key. The reporting on it is careful: the change is operational, not regulatory. Ownership, tax, and liability rules still apply, and a responsible human or organisation is still on the hook.

**Multi-agent organisations of meaningful scale.** Five to twenty agents working concurrently under a Paperclip-style control plane, with budgets and approvals, are demonstrably possible in 2026. Whether they hold up over months, in production, with adversarial users, is still being learned in public.

**A genuine one-person billion-dollar company.** Sam Altman has predicted it since 2024; Dario Amodei publicly predicted in 2026 that the first one might appear by year-end. The most-cited reference case, Medvi (Matthew Gallagher&apos;s GLP-1 telehealth business), reportedly hit $401M of 2025 sales — but later hired family and contractors, depended on third-party medical and pharmacy partners, and surfaced real failure modes (chatbot fabrications about pricing, unverifiable products, FDA letters, marketing-related complaints). It is a strong signal, not a clean proof.

## What is still narrative

**A company that, in any defensible legal or fiduciary sense, runs without a responsible human or organisation.** Company registration, KYC/AML, banking, tax, contracts, and liability all assume an identifiable responsible party. The ClawBank-style experiments make agent ownership operationally smoother but do not change that constraint. &quot;Zero human&quot; in the strict sense is not what the working implementations actually claim; the more honest description is human-supervised autonomous operations.

**Stable multi-quarter agent execution without a control plane.** The frontier agent task horizon has grown impressively; the failure mode is no longer one-shot reasoning, it is drift over weeks, context loss across sessions, and inconsistent decisions as state accumulates. This is the bottleneck that Paperclip-style tooling is trying to remove, but it is not yet a solved problem.

**Agent organisations operating in heavily regulated industries without structural human-in-the-loop requirements.** Finance, healthcare, legal — the human approval is in the regulation, not in the technology. AI helps the human decide faster; it does not remove the requirement.

## What I am personally watching

This is opinion, marked as such.

I think the Paperclip-shaped product category — agent organisation control planes — is where the next year of compounding progress lives. Building one more coding agent is no longer the bottleneck; managing a small organisation of them inside policy is.

I think the boundary between &quot;one-person company&quot; and &quot;zero-human company&quot; will keep moving toward the high-leverage end as control planes mature. The working answer for most founders in 2026 is the AI-native one-person company mode — founder as orchestrator — not the aspirational zero-human mode.

I think the moats will move quickly. When every founder gets the same AI starter kit, the differentiators are vertical know-how, distribution and trust, proprietary data, taste, and compliance edges. &quot;I have AI tools&quot; is not a moat; &quot;I have AI tools and a thing the agents cannot regenerate&quot; is.

I will keep updating this page as the constraints move. The list of &quot;still narrative&quot; gets shorter every quarter. None of it is shrinking to empty.
