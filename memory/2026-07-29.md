submodule advanced to actual upstream `main`. For gbrain: `git checkout

Take-away: when a submodule shows dirty on a cold worktree, always
`git ls-tree HEAD <path>` and `git rev-parse origin/<default>` before
deciding whether to bump or reset — direction can differ per submodule
in a single session.
