Abstract
We study the relations under Weihrauch reducibility of the well-ordering preservation principle for the operator X 7→ Xω and the Ordered Ramsey Theorem. Both principles are known to be equivalent to Σ0
2-induction in Reverse Mathematics. We show that the Ordered Ramsey Theorem is Weihrauch-equivalent to the parallel product of the well-ordering preservation principle for the operator X 7→ Xω and the Eventually Constant Tail principle. By previous work from Pauly, Pradic and Solda`, the Ordered Ramsey Theorem is known to be Weihrauchequivalent to the parallel product of the Eventually Constant Tail principle and the parallelization of the jump of the Limited Principle of Omniscience. We show that-
 the latter pinciple and the well-ordering preservation principle for X 7→ Xω are Weihrauch-incomparable.
1 Introduction and Motivation
A well-ordering preservation principle (or well-ordering principle – WOP for short) states that some operator on linear orderings preserves well-ordering. A Ramsey-type theorem states that some class of colorings is partitionregular. Both families of principles are well investigated in Reverse Mathematics [5]. Many prominent systems can be characterized in terms of WOPs (see [11, 9]) and of Ramsey-type theorems (see [5]). Usually the equivalence of the latter two is obtained as an indirect by-prod-
uct. We refer to [2] for examples and discussion. In the present paper we study from the viewpoint of Weihrauch reducibility the well-ordering principle for the operator X 7→ Xω and the Ordered Ramsey Theorem. This is one way of making precise the question from [3] whether direct proofs exist, in one direction or the other, between Ramsey-type principles and WOPs. The motivation for the present work is twofold. First, we continue the line of research initiated in [2] concerning the possibility of -
establishing Weihrauch reductions of well-ordering preservation principles to combinatorial theorems from Ramsey Theory. For example it is shown in [2] that the WOP for the operator X 7→ ωX is Weihrauch reducible to Ramsey’s Theorem for 2-colorings of 3-subsets of N as well as to Hindman’s Theorem restricted to finite sums of 3 terms. The initial motivation for the present work comes from the observation in [2] that a similar proof shows that the WOP for the operator X 7→ Xω reduces to Ramsey’s Th-
eorem for finite colorings of 2-subsets. The latter result is not optimal from the point of view of Reverse Mathematics, since the WOP in question is known to be equivalent to Σ02-induction [12], while Ramsey’s Theorem for pairs
and arbitrarily many colors implies the stronger principle of Σ03-bounding [7]. It was therefore natural to ask
whether the same proof-technique from [2] could be applied to reduce the WOP for X 7→ Xω to a Ramsey-type statement known to be equivalent to Σ02. The Ordered Ramsey Theorem introduced in [8] is one such candidate. The fact that the latter principle was studied from the point of view of Weihrauch complexity in [10] has been our main motivation for focusing, among the known combinatorial principles equivalent to Σ02-induction, on the Ordered Ramsey Theorem. The analysis in [10] gave some indication-
 on how to obtain reductions in the direction, from the WOP for X 7→ Xω to the Ordered Ramsey Theorem. Note that reductions from WOPs to Ramsey-type statements are not to be found in the previous literature.

2.3 Ramsey-type theorems
We here introduce the Ramsey-type principles of interest to the present paper, i.e., the Ordered and the Additive Ramsey Theorems. Fix a linear order (X, <X ). For every poset (P, <P ), we call a colouring c : [X]2 → P right-ordered if we have c(x, y) ≤P c(x, y′) when x <X y ≤X y′.
Definition (Ordered Ramsey Theorem). The problem ORTN takes as input a finite poset (P, ≤P ) and a rightordered colouring c : [N]2 → P , and outputs an infinite c-homogeneous set ⊆ N.
Definition (Additive Ramsey Theorem). The problem ARTN takes as input a finite semigroup S and an additive colouring c : [N]2 → S, and outputs an infinite c-homogeneous set ⊆ N.
In Reverse Mathematics the ∀∃ theorems naturally corresponding to ORTN and ARTN in Second-Order Arithmetic were introduced in [8] and proved equivalent to Σ02-induction over RCA0. The Weihrauch complexity of the problems ORTN and ARTN is studied in [10]. Finally we will make use of the principle called Eventually Constant Tail, introduced in [4].
Definition (Eventually Constant Tail). ECT is the problem whose instances are pairs (n, f ) ∈ N × NN such that f : N → n is a colouring of the natural numbers with n colours, and such that, for every instance (n, f ) and b ∈ N, b ∈ ECT(n, f ) if and only if ∀x > b∃y > x(f (x) = f (y)).
In other words, ECT is the problem that, upon being given a function f of the integers with finite range, outputs a b such that, after that b, the palette of colours used is constant (hence its name, which stands for eventually constant palette tail). In Reverse Mathematics the principle corresponding to ECT is equivalent to Σ02-induction [4].

5 Conclusion and perspectives
We have compared in terms of Weirauch reducibility the Ordered Ramsey Theorem ORTN and the Well-Ordering Preservation Principle for ω-exponentiation WOP(X 7→ Xω). The fact that the Ordered Ramsey Theorem is reducible to the parallel product of ECT and WOP(X 7→ Xω) is the first example of a Weihrauch reduction of a combinatorial principle to a well-ordering preservation principle. While we obtained a complete picture concerning the principles studied in this paper, many question remains for other p-
rinciples of interest. We consider the present work as a specimen of a wider study of Weihrauch reducibilities between WOPs and Ramsey-type theorems. We believe that the approach used in the present paper can be successfully applied to many other principles of strength around that of Σ02-induction. We believe that the reductions from WOPs can be useful to obtain analogous results also at higher level of logical complexity, e.g., for the principles studied in [2].
