1. Introduction
Let μ be a Borel probability measure on Rn and ⟨·⟩ denote the standard inner product on Rn. It is said that μ is a spectral measure if there exists a countable set Λ ⊂ Rn such that EΛ := {e−2πi⟨λ,x⟩ : λ ∈ Λ} forms an orthonormal basis for L2(μ). In this case, the set Λ is called a spectrum of μ. If the normalized Lebesgue measure restricting on a domain Ω ⊂ Rn is a spectral measure, then we say Ω is a spectral set. The study of spectral measures originated from Fuglede conjecture [8]. Fuglede conj-
